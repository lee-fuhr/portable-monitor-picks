Frequently asked questions

What refresh rate should a gaming portable monitor have?

For portable gaming monitors, 120Hz or higher is the standard worth targeting. The jump from 60Hz to 120Hz is clearly visible in fast-paced games, making motion feel smoother and more responsive. Some portable gaming monitors now reach 144Hz, matching the sweet spot of desktop gaming monitors. Going above 144Hz on a portable is less common and drains your laptop battery faster, so 120 to 144Hz is the practical range to look for.

Can I use a portable gaming monitor for console gaming?

Yes. Most portable gaming monitors include an HDMI input alongside USB-C, which lets you connect a PS5, Xbox, or Nintendo Switch directly. They make an especially good travel companion for the Switch, which becomes a portable gaming station with a proper monitor instead of the small built-in screen. Check that the monitor supports the HDMI version your console uses for the best output quality.

Does a portable gaming monitor drain my laptop battery faster?

Yes. Running a gaming workload through your GPU at the same time as powering the monitor through your USB-C port is demanding on the battery. Expect meaningfully shorter runtime compared to gaming on the laptop screen alone. If battery longevity matters, look for a monitor with its own power input as a fallback so you can wall-power the display and preserve laptop battery for the GPU load.
